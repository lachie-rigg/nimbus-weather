# ☀️ Nimbus Weather ☀️

Nimbus Weather is a web platform deidcated to the Australian public. It is intended to fix the many gripes of modern weather apps and provide a beautiful interface with a focus on accurate, up to date and extensive weather data. Suggestions, feedback and requests are always welcome.

This platform is written with a React frontend and .NET/C# backend. 

# UI/UX

Nimbus' main focus is to provide a much simpler and easy to understand interface than modern weather apps have today. Other weather platforms often feel convoluted, clunky and flat out not very attractive. Nimbus' focus is to provide clear, readable and aesthetic weather data for the user while being a pleasure to navigate. 

# Weather Data

Aussie weather data is notoriously unpredictable (looking at you Melbourne). Our most trusted source of weather data is from the ever reliable BOM. That is why Nimbus will predominately utilise the WillyWeather API, for trusted BOM sourced weather data. Through development, Nimbus will include everything from tides to aurora activity for all your meteorological needs.
